What to expect, and recovery

An honest walk-through of an adult brit milah: before, the day itself, the first days, and getting back to normal.

From first call to full recovery

  1. Before

    We talk it through, your mohel goes over your medical history with you, and you agree on a pain plan. You know the date, the place and what to bring.

  2. The day

    During the day, in a private office or a place we arrange together. Local anesthesia means the area is numb while it’s done. The brit is followed by the blessing.

  3. The first days

    Expect soreness; the pain plan is already in place. You go home with clear written aftercare instructions.

  4. Back to normal

    Most men are back to desk work within a few days. Heavier physical work and exercise wait longer, and we plan around your job.

You’re not on your own

Recovery is where many men feel most alone. You won’t be. You’ll have a direct number, and your mohel checks in with you through the healing period. No question is too small.

If something worries you, call. In an emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room first, then call us.

Planning around your life

Tell us about your work and your schedule, and we’ll choose a date that fits. Read more about adult brit milah, cost, or whether it’s too late.

Short answers

How long is recovery after an adult brit milah?

Most men are back to desk work within a few days, with a longer wait before strenuous activity. Full healing takes a few weeks. You’ll get a realistic picture for your own situation.

Does it hurt afterwards?

There’s some soreness in the first days. Pain management is planned before we begin, not improvised, and you’ll be told honestly what to expect.

When should I call?

Any time something worries you, however small. In an emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room first, then call us.
