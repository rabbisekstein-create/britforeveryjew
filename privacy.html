Privacy

What you share with us stays private. Here is exactly what we keep, and who sees it.

What we collect

This website has no forms and asks you for nothing. You reach us by phone or WhatsApp, and we keep only what you choose to tell us in those conversations, so we can call you back, answer your questions and, if you decide to go ahead, arrange your brit safely.

There are no advertising trackers, analytics or cookies on this site.

Who sees it

Only the people helping you with your brit. If a rabbi needs to look at your family background, we ask you first and share only that part. Your medical details are shared only with your permission, or in an emergency.

Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for marketing.

Your choices

You can ask us what we have, correct it, or have it deleted, at any time. Call or message 914-734-5364.
