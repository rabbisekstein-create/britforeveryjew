Two situations, one covenant

Most men who reach out to us are in one of these two places. Either way, the first step is just a conversation.

I never had a brit

Many Jewish men were never circumcised — because of where they grew up, family circumstances, or simply because no one asked. A brit at any age is done privately by an experienced, certified mohel under local anesthesia.

Some men prefer it completely discreet. Others celebrate afterwards with family or their rabbi. That choice is yours.

I was circumcised in a hospital

If you were circumcised by a doctor rather than at a brit, you can still complete your brit. For most men this is hatafat dam brit: just a quick pinprick to draw a single drop of blood, with the blessings. No surgery and no recovery time.

Every situation is a little different, so we start with a private conversation to understand yours and explain exactly what's involved.

How it works

No forms to fill out first, and no commitment until you're ready.

  1. Reach out

    Call or send a WhatsApp message. Ask anything — no question is too small.

  2. Talk it through

  3. Choose a date

    Done privately and carefully, followed by a celebration if you'd like one.
