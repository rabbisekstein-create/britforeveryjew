Ask a question, anonymously

Some questions are easier to type than to say out loud. Ask anything about brit milah, hatafat dam brit, recovery or what applies to you. No name is needed.

General information only. Every case is decided by a rabbi.

How it works

Type your question below. You don’t need to give your name, email address or phone number. Please leave out anything that could identify you.

The simplest way to get an answer is a private code. After you send your question, you’ll see a code. Come back in a few days, enter it, and you’ll find our answer. You can reply and keep the conversation going, still without giving your name.

If you prefer, you can ask for an answer by WhatsApp or email instead, or ask without any reply. With your permission, we may publish the question and our answer on this site, with every identifying detail removed, so others can learn from it.
