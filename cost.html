Learn / Cost

Cost is never the reason to wait

Every Jew deserves a brit, and money shouldn’t stand in the way. If cost is a concern, sponsorship is available. Just ask.

How it works

On the first call, before anything is arranged, we tell you plainly what your situation involves and what it costs, so you’re never guessing. If cost is a real obstacle, say so. We talk about it privately, and where it’s needed, a sponsor covers it.

Nobody else needs to know that a sponsor was involved. It’s arranged quietly, and it stays between us.

What’s included

  • Private conversations by phone or WhatsApp, as many as you need
  • Going through your medical history with you before anything is scheduled
  • The brit itself, under local anesthesia, with a pain plan agreed in advance
  • Sterile, single-use instruments
  • Clear written aftercare instructions
  • A direct number, and follow-up through the whole healing period

A hatafat dam brit is a much smaller procedure, and involves much less.

Short answers

Yes. Talking it through costs nothing and commits you to nothing.

Tell us when we speak. We’ll talk about it privately, and where it’s needed, a sponsor covers the cost.

No. It’s arranged quietly and stays between us.
